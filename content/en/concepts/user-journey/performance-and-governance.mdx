## Viewing portfolio performance

1. Open the **Performance** section. Your gardens are listed.
2. Select a garden, then select a time range.
3. The application loads the historical data and displays portfolio value, asset allocation, profit and loss, and performance charts.
4. Changing the garden or the time range reloads the data in place.

## Voting on a proposal

1. Open the **Governance** section. Active and completed proposals are listed.
2. Filter or search, then select a proposal.
3. The application shows the proposal details, the actions it proposes, and the current vote breakdown.
4. The application checks whether you are eligible to vote.
5. Select **Yes**, **No**, or **Abstain**, and review your selection.
6. Confirm, then approve the transaction in your wallet.
7. The application submits your vote and shows its status until confirmed.
8. The proposal's vote count refreshes and the updated status is shown.

Voting power comes from how much $BLOKC you hold. Note that contributor rewards still count while locked, so locked $BLOKC carries voting power from day one. See [Contributor Rewards](/en/resources/rewards-program) for how that works.

### When you cannot vote

The application checks the proposal status and your eligibility before letting you vote, and explains which of these applies:

- The proposal is closed.
- You have already voted on it.
- You have no voting power.

## Account settings

1. Open **Settings**. The Account section is shown.
2. Review your account information.
3. Open the security or backup options.
4. Complete the requested MFA or backup-factor step.
5. The application verifies it and shows your updated account status.

## About and legal

Both live under **Settings**. The **About** tab shows application information. The **Legal** tab shows the available legal information.
