## Gardens don't have a fixed address

A Garden isn't listed here because there isn't one address to list. Gardens are deployed per user by the GardenFactory at a deterministic address (CREATE2), up to 10 per user, and ownership of each one is an ERC-721 token. To find a specific Garden, query the factory rather than looking for it on this page.

## Verifying an address

BLOK Capital only publishes addresses in two places: this page and the [public audits repository](https://github.com/BLOKCapital/audits). Anything posted elsewhere, including in chat channels or direct messages, should be assumed hostile until you have matched it against one of those two sources.

Two checks worth doing yourself before you transact:

- Open the Arbiscan link and confirm the contract has code and matches the name above.
- For a facet, call `facets()` on the Garden you care about (via DiamondLoupeFacet) and confirm the facet address the Garden actually routes to is the one listed here. A facet being deployed doesn't mean a given Garden uses it.
